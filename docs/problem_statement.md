# Problem Statement

University students often cannot tell how well their current skills match industry job requirements, or which missing skills they should learn first.

Recruiters lack a structured and transparent way to find candidates based on the skills a job requires.

Placement departments lack data on the gap between the skills employers demand and the skills students actually hold.

## Example

A student knows Python, SQL, Advanced Excel and Tableau. A Data Analyst job requires Python, SQL, Excel, Power BI and Statistics. The student cannot easily see which skills are missing, which to learn first, or how well they fit the role.

## What SkillSync Does

SkillSync is a relational database-driven system that connects student skills, projects, certifications and assessments with job requirements. It calculates explainable skill-coverage scores, identifies and prioritizes missing skills, keeps a history of past calculations, and gives placement administrators analytics on skill demand versus student skill supply.

## Important Note

The match score is a skill-coverage indicator. It is not a probability of getting hired.
